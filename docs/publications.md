## Beyond flare science

These datasets have applications outside flare physics. The campaigns
also produce high-cadence, high-resolution observations of active
regions and of general plasma evolution, and the Tan et al. (2025) result
came from a window in which no major flare occurred.

The campaigns have also fed into instrument development: they have been
central to building the science and technical case for the
High-resolution Flare Imager (HIFI), a flare-focused EUV imager proposed
as part of the **SPARK** mission concept to ESA's M-class call.
