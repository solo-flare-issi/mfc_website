## What is this site for?

Solar Orbiter's remote-sensing instruments observe flares only during
short, carefully planned campaign windows. This site is the public record
of those campaigns:

- **Documenting how the campaigns run.** The observing modes, the
  target-selection process, and the flare-trigger strategies that let
  EUI and PHI respond to flares as they happen.
- **Cataloguing what was observed.** Every campaign window since 2023,
  and every flare captured within one, in a machine-readable form.
- **Pointing to the data.** Where each dataset lives in SOAR and the
  instrument archives, and how to find coordinated observations from
  IRIS, Hinode, DKIST, radio arrays and ground-based observatories.
- **Tracking the science.** The papers that come out of these
  campaigns, linked to the events they use.

:::{note}
The campaign and flare catalogues are placeholders while the underlying
catalogue is finalised. The narrative pages are live.
:::
