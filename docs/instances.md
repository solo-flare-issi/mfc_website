# MFC-SOOP instances

Every formal Major Flare Campaign SOOP instance run since 2024, organised
by season, with the coordinators for each campaign credited.

Windows are the planned windows in UT; `(+1d)` marks a window running
past midnight. Instruments listed are those with data in SOAR under the
Major Flare SOOP. For actual observed start and end times, per-instrument
file counts and verified cadences, see the campaign detail pages.

:::{note} Draft
Coordinator lists and per-season narratives are drawn from the team's
working documents and need a review pass. Flare columns will link
through to the [flare catalogue](flares/index.md) once it is published.
:::


## Future campaigns

Solar Orbiter's orbit has changed, and there is no Major Flare Campaign
scheduled for autumn 2026. We expect the SOOP to run again in a future
Remote Sensing Window, most likely in summer 2027 during LTP28, with the
exact windows to be determined.

The most recent instances ran in August and September 2026 and are listed
under [Summer 2026 (LTP24)](#summer-2026-ltp24) below.

## Spring 2023 (LTP11), early coordinated campaign

**Coordinators:** Therese Kucera, Andrew Inglis

One instance on 2023-04-05, SPICE-only from Solar Orbiter with no EUI,
supported by extensive Earth-side coordination: IRIS (3 sequences) and
Hinode HOP 476 (EIS ×3, SOT-SP, XRT ×2). This window pre-dates the formal
MFC SOOP but establishes the coordination pattern the campaigns inherited.

The SPICE slit was reportedly not on the active region, so the scientific
value of this window is limited, it is recorded for completeness.

## Spring 2024 (LTP15), first full MFC-SOOP season

**Coordinators:** Daniel Ryan, Laura Hayes, Hannah Collier, Andrew Inglis

The first full season (RSW 13 and 14), and a very successful one.
22 flares between C and M class were observed jointly by STIX and
EUI/HRI<sub>EUV</sub>, along with several B-class events. Four flares
were co-observed by STIX, EUI/HRI<sub>EUV</sub> and SPICE together,
though the SPICE slit mostly crossed flare loops rather than footpoints;
two events below C class did have footpoint coverage.

Most instances ran the baseline 16 s cycle with six short exposures at
2 s cadence. IRIS and EIS coordination was in place, with EOVSA also
available. The season coincided with the FOXSI-4 / Hi-C Flare rocket
campaign.

**Key events.** The 2024-03-19 M2.2 shows clear ribbon footpoints. The
2024-03-23 M2.5 caught dynamic ribbons under a top-down viewing geometry,
part of a sequence with an M2.7, an M2.2 and two C-class flares across
2024-03-23/24. Several April 2024 flares were far-side from Earth and
captured only by STIX, with classes estimated in the B3-M1 range.

**Earth-side coordination.** EOVSA, IRIS, Hinode (XRT and EIS-plus), DKIST
Cryo-NIRSP on the west limb for 2024-03-23, and BBSO Hα.

**Known issues.** For 2024-04-03 only PHI is listed in SOAR under the MFC
SOOP, and SPICE ran in raster mode rather than flare sit-and-stare.

## Autumn 2024 (LTP17)

**Coordinators:** Daniel Ryan, Laura Hayes, Hannah Collier, Andrew Inglis

| Instance | Date | Window (UT) | EUI cadence | Instruments in SOAR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MFW20241015A | 2024-10-15 | 15:00 - 00:00 (+1d) | 2 s | EUI, SPICE, PHI |

A single instance (RSW 17), with HRI running 18:00-22:00 inside a wider
SPICE and STIX window. Several flares were caught by
EUI/HRI<sub>EUV</sub> and STIX, and others by STIX and SPICE, but not yet
any single event with all three instruments together. It captured an **M2.1 with a strong non-thermal
X-ray component**, two main X-ray footpoints and EUV substructure, the
headline event of the season. Coordination included IRIS (5 sequences),
Hinode SOT/EIS/XRT, and BBSO Hα.

This season also included a substantial set of high-cadence EUI windows
that were not formal MFC SOOPs, including the ten-window December 2024
block. Those are catalogued as MFC-related windows.

## Spring 2025 (LTP19)

**Coordinators:** Hannah Collier, Laura Hayes, Andrew Inglis

| Instance | Date | Window (UT) | EUI cadence | Instruments in SOAR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MFW20250314A | 2025-03-14 | 16:10 - 23:10 | 3 s | EUI, SPICE, PHI |
| MFW20250315A | 2025-03-15 | 12:00 - 16:00 | 2 s | EUI, SPICE, PHI |
| MFW20250330A | 2025-03-30 | 11:00 - 15:30 | 2 s | EUI, SPICE, PHI |
| MFW20250402A | 2025-04-02 | 05:00 - 09:30 | 2 s | EUI, SPICE, PHI |

Four instances across RSW 19 and 20. The first trialled a new 60 s cycle
with 18 shorts at 3 s plus one short at 2 s; later instances reverted to
the 16 s baseline.

A small eruption was co-observed by EUI/HRI and SPICE at around 21:30 UT
on 2025-03-14. The remaining instances saw no significant
HRI<sub>EUV</sub> flare, but still produced coordinated SPICE
sit-and-stare and flare-context raster datasets.

This is the first season with detailed SPICE observation records in the
catalogue, line lists, MISOID identifiers, raster windows and
sit-and-stare intervals.

**Coordinators:** Andrew Inglis, Graham Kerr

| Instance | Date | Window (UT) | EUI cadence | Instruments in SOAR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MFW20260212A | 2026-02-12 | 17:00 - 21:25 | 2 s | EUI, SPICE |

| MFW20260228A | 2026-02-28 | 20:05 - 00:25 (+1d) | 2 s | EUI, SPICE, PHI |

Three instances across RSW 25 and 26, with coordination from IRIS, EIS
and Big Bear Solar Observatory, the latter observing out of season for
us. One instance in RSW 26 used an alternative SPICE line list covering
lines useful for FIP-effect studies.

The 12 and 13 February instances had no flares during the campaign
window. Riblets and a weak, brief B-class event were noted in IRIS/SJI on
2026-02-12.

These windows overlapped partially with the PHI trigger campaign. The two
X-class events of early February 2026 were captured by PHI's FDT during
the trigger block, outside these formal SOOP windows, see
[Trigger Campaigns](trigger-campaigns.md).
